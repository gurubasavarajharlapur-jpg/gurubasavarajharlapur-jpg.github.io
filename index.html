Middlesex University Dubai · 2026 · MSc dissertation

Early detection of foot & nail conditions

Four-class classification of foot and nail images for rural clinics where the specialist who would catch a diabetic foot ulcer early isn't there. A controlled comparison: a phone-sized model against a full-size one, to price what portability costs.

  • 8,374 images, four public sources, 1,758 near-duplicates removed before splitting; 1,248 test images, evaluated once.
  • Same code path and splits for both backbones: MobileNetV2 98.32% vs ResNet50 98.96% accuracy.
  • The 0.64-point gap is not significant (McNemar, p = 0.15) — MobileNetV2 gets there at a tenth of the parameters and 1.9× faster on CPU.
  • Calibrated confidence with abstention: decline the least confident 3%, be right on 99.6% of the rest. Grad-CAM and occlusion tests confirm it reads pathology, not dataset provenance.
Recommendation

MobileNetV2 for phone screening: the accuracy gap is small and bounded — the size and latency gap is not.

AI agent · Workflow automation · 2026

A/B Test Workflow

An A/B test normally starts with a seven-team kickoff meeting, and the problems surface late. In this prototype a CRO submits one brief, an AI drafts one test spec, the spec is checked, and all five teams approve their own section at the same time — tracked through six stages on a dashboard.

  • An n8n workflow drafts an eight-section spec. An input check stops an incomplete brief before the AI ever sees it; an output check then verifies every section, that the metric matches the brief and that no claim was dropped — a bad draft gets one retry, then goes to a person. Nothing is passed on silently.
  • Claims are checked against an approved library, so Legal is asked only when a claim is new, and only about that claim. All five approval requests go out at once, teams decide in any order, and every decision is logged with its reviewer and timestamp in the final spec.
  • Node.js dashboard over SQLite: a six-stage tracker, an approvals inbox showing which team owes what, counts computed from stored records rather than typed in, and a downloadable final spec. Runs offline in mock mode and works down to 390px.

AI agent · Full-stack · 2026

Operation Agents

Vendor invoices arrive by webhook and an agent works each case: validate, match to the purchase order, then propose a payment, query the vendor, or escalate. The agent can only propose — a named human approves every payment.

  • TypeScript throughout — Fastify API, React dashboard, worker — with one Postgres holding the business tables, the job queue and an append-only hash-chained audit trail, so a case and its job commit in the same transaction.
  • Least privilege by design: the worker connects as a role that cannot approve anything.
  • 32 hand-labelled eval cases graded from the database end state — no model grades anything — plus 109 tests and 5 Playwright browser tests in CI.
